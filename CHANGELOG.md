# Changelog

## 0.9.0

- **Draft changes on the pictures.** **✎ Draft changes** opens a toolbar at the bottom of the window, on maps, data models, flows (including a plan's own steps), state, decision, impact and Kubernetes pictures: add boxes, zones, tables, columns, foreign keys, groups, lanes and lines (with label and kind), rename, take out, drag between zones, groups or lanes; undo and redo; keys V B Z L R D (C for a column), Delete, Enter, Escape. Every change is drawn on the picture with an outline and one sign (+ ~ − ↦), listed in the side panel, and kept in `.walkboard/draft.json`, never in plan.json; sketching never wakes the agent. **Ask the agent to review** sends the changes with names next to ids, and the agent puts the agreed ones in the plan. Two pages sketching at once, a stopped server or a moment when plan.json cannot be read lose nothing: saves carry a version, merge, and retry. `walkboard draft <dir> [clear …]` prints or clears the draft. Exports, PDF and before / after pictures show the plan without the draft.
- **Easier to sketch on a map.** Each tool says what to do in a line over the toolbar, and the zone a new box or zone would go into lights up under the pointer (the cursor says where nothing can go). A box is added and edited in one card: name, a note for whoever reads the plan (`what`, up to 300 characters) and the icon, which follows the name until you pick one. Signs say what they mean (**+ new**, **~ edited**, **− out**, **↦ moved**). A note such as "a zone is already called …" goes by itself. The picture's frame no longer loses its sides, lines follow while a new box or zone is being named, and a new zone lands where you clicked.
- A note that a change needs draft mode (a line joined to another box, a box dropped into another zone, a table dropped into another group) has a **Draft changes** button that opens it.
- A box or zone moved by hand never ends up covering another zone: when the picture reflows (a draft zone dropped, a window of another width) it goes back to its place in the plan, and one let go over another zone (not into it) slides back.
- **Auto or by hand, on a map.** A switch over the picture: **Auto** lays it out as before; **By hand** keeps every box and zone where it is, so adding, moving, taking out or dropping something into another zone moves nothing else. A new box or zone goes where you click (clear of the boxes there), and zones grow right and down to hold what you put in them. Saved in `layout` (`"@fixed"`, places from the corner of what holds each one); switching back to Auto lays it out again. Whatever moved into another zone blinks where it landed.
- **What the map learned, on the other pictures.** State, decision, impact, Kubernetes and flow pictures add and edit boxes in the same card (name, note, icon; `addBox` can carry `what` and `icon`); their lines turn round from the card (`reverseEdge`, `reverseLink` on Kubernetes), light up with their label when pointed at, and the edit and take-out tools act on them. Every picture's toolbar says what each tool does, the lane (flow) or group frame (data model) a click would use lights up, and the draft frame keeps its sides. A Kubernetes line turned round is routed around the boxes instead of across them.
- **How to read this picture.** State, decision, impact, flow and data model pictures have a **? How to read this picture** line: what an arrow and its words mean there (a transition and its event, an answer, a dependency, lanes in order, foreign keys and groups) and what the draft signs mean.
- **How to read the lines.** A **?** after the line legend (maps, Kubernetes) opens a card: which way an arrow points (who starts the connection), what the label on a box means, each kind of line, what the draft colours mean, and that a line may take another route when it is redrawn.
- **Turn a line round.** The card of a line has **⇄ Reverse**: the arrow points the other way and keeps its label and kind (on a map the label moves to the box it now reaches). A line being drawn just swaps its ends; one already in the plan becomes a `reverseLink` change, and the agent is told who now starts the connection. Pointing at a line in draft mode thickens it and lights up its label; the edit tool opens a line's card and the take-out tool takes a line out.
- **A zone's name, note and icon** are edited in the same card as a box's (the edit tool, or double-click its title), and a new zone is named there too: `addZone` and `editZone` can carry `icon` and `note` (`""` takes one away), and `editZone` no longer needs a new name.
- The layout switch is two icons (the words are in their tooltips). Switching back to **Auto** keeps the picture as it is; only what changes next makes room. The page keeps its place when the picture is drawn again (a drop, a switch, undo) instead of jumping to the top.
- **Smaller and quicker.** Lines are tested against boxes by exact clipping instead of a point every 6 px, and rounded corners are cut into fewer pieces: the line work in a redraw of the 52-box map takes about a quarter of the time. The page is sent without whole-line comments, indentation or blank lines (421 → 360 KB; modules 86 → 74 KB, exports likewise), and built once per change of its files instead of on every request.
- **Resize a zone** from its corner (it shows when you point at the zone); double-click the corner for its own size. In Auto it is the zone's smallest size, never so small that what it holds spills out. Saved as `w`, `h` in `layout`.
- A zone let go on empty space comes out of the zone that held it, to the top of the picture (a draft change, like dropping it into another zone). A box let go outside every zone slides back and says it needs a zone: before, both only looked moved while the plan kept them where they were.
- A box dropped into a zone sketched after it, and a line drawn to that box, wait for the zone instead of no longer fitting.
- **Ask the agent to review a map.** After you arrange or sketch on a map, **Ask the agent to review** sends where every box and zone sits on screen (no screenshot, nothing saved) and the draft. The agent says what each change would mean for the plan and changes the plan only if you agree. Dragging still never wakes it.
- **Drop a box into another zone.** On a map, a box or zone dropped into another zone moves into it (the zone grows to hold it) and is marked as not in the plan yet; the zone it will land in lights up while you drag. Review it with the agent or undo. Its old place stays as an empty outline, so the zone it left keeps its size and nothing around it moves. It is a draft change, kept in `.walkboard/draft.json` (never in plan.json), and it goes once the plan moves the box.
- **Lay out automatically** is now **Reset to the plan**, which is what it does.
- A box or zone dragged past the edge of a map stops there, and one saved off screen comes back to the edge.
- **Smoother big maps.** Dragging redraws only the lines that touch what moves, a redraw is skipped when nothing moved, and routing tests each path only against nearby boxes: on a 52-box map a drag frame went from about 53 ms to 4 ms. `scripts/perf.mjs` measures it.
- A map offset in `layout` remembers the zone it was dragged in (`"in"`). When the plan moves the box to another zone, the old offset is dropped instead of pushing the box away from its new place.
- **A data model holds still while you sketch.** A new table stays where you clicked (in a group's frame: in that group), nudged only as far as it takes not to lie on another table, and no change to the draft moves what is already on the picture: the layout is kept from the first change on, one undo with it. A table that grows as you add columns moves what it would reach down. A table with no foreign keys no longer pulls its group to the first column, and one the agent just added starts on free ground.
- **In and out of a data model's groups.** While sketching, a table let go outside its group's frame leaves the group (`moveTable` with `to: ""`), and the frame stays with the tables left in it; back over the frame, the change drops out. A table let go over a group lands beside the others instead of on one of them, where the frame takes in no table of another group. A table alone in its frame still takes the frame along.
- A name already in use on a data model (a table, a column of that table, a group; capitals do not count) is said so, instead of the change quietly doing nothing; a new column's field comes back with what was typed.
- Dragging a foreign key on a data model scrolls the picture when held near an edge of the window, so the column it points at can be a screen away.
- A column renamed in a draft shows only its new name; the old one is in the tooltip, so the table does not grow for it.
- **A table has the same card as a box.** On a data model, adding or editing a table opens the card a map box has: name, note and icon, with the table on the picture following what you type. `addTable` and `editNode` carry `what` and `icon`, and a table shows its note under its name. Columns are still typed in place (`name type pk`).
- A box in several lanes of a flow (steps that touch the same box) moves out of the lane it is dragged from: reordering it in one step no longer adds a second copy there or takes it out of another step. A lane that already has the box takes no second one, and the agent is told which lane (step) a box left (`moveBox` `fromLane`, `fromLaneTitle`).
- A box or lane dragged on a flow floats over the page instead of going behind the lane frame below it.
- **A group looks and edits like a zone.** A data model's group has a frame and a title like a map zone's, and a card for its name, note and icon (`groups[].note`, `groups[].icon`; `addGroup` and `editGroup` carry them).
- **The same card everywhere.** A lane is added and renamed in a card (only a name: it has no note or icon), and on state, decision, impact and Kubernetes pictures a new box shows where you clicked as you type, as on a map.
- **The cursor says where nothing can go, on every picture.** The tools that place something show a no-entry cursor over what they cannot use (a table or box under a new one, empty space for the column and group tools, the gap between lanes), as does dragging a box out of every lane or a table onto another. A table let go on another goes back, with a line that says why.
- Draft mode reads the same across pictures: the tools come in a map's order (what to add, lines, edit, take out), what a click or a drop would land in lights up the same way, a table's sign sits on its corner as a box's does, and where the page places the boxes the first tool no longer says "drag".
- **Primary keys at a click.** While sketching a data model, the slot before a column's name turns its primary key on and off (a foreign key stays one), and shows **PK** under the pointer. A column the draft added can now lose its type or key by typing it without them.
- **A data model's lines can be clicked.** A line opens a card, as on a map: which columns it joins, N:1 or 1:1 (`editRelation`), and take it out (`removeRelation` for one in `relations`). The edit and take-out tools work on lines, a line thickens under the pointer, and dragging a column onto another moves its line.
- A group the draft added and that has no table left leaves the draft on its own, and a table in such a group can move to another group or out of it (before, the move was refused as no longer fitting the plan).
- **Draft mode is where changes happen.** **Ask the agent to review** is only in draft mode. Outside it a drag only arranges: a box or table let go over another zone or group goes back and says to use draft mode, undo only moves boxes back, and **Reset to the plan** is now **Arrange again**, which is what it does: the page arranges the picture from the plan as it is now, and the draft stays. It is not offered on before / after, where it cleared the positions of another tab. **✎ Draft changes** is also on before / after, in the same place: it offers to edit either picture and opens it in draft mode. A kind of picture with no draft has no button.
- Dragging a table for the first time on a page scrolled down no longer jumps the page to the top.
- **Start over from the plan.** A button on draft mode's toolbar and in its list drops the draft and the positions of the picture, after asking; undo brings both back. It takes the place of **Drop the whole draft**, which left the positions and was two menus away.
- Drawing a line on any picture scrolls it when held near an edge of the window, not only on a data model.
- Two lanes cannot take one name in a draft: changes find a lane by its title, so they would be mixed up.
- A kind's fields show their names where the value alone says little ("replicas 3", "cidr 10.0.1.0/24"; "postgres" stays as it is), tags use short names (VLAN, VM, ALB, PVC…) and leave out what the name of the box or zone already says, long values wrap inside the box, fields take at most 80 characters, and a warning sign is one line (all of it on hover).
- Kinds now warn across platforms too (an Azure SQL in a Google Cloud subnet, a Deployment in a physical server), except where the zone runs that platform: containers on an EC2, a VM or a server; Kubernetes in EKS, GKE, AKS or CCE. Designs that are normal no longer warn (Cloud SQL, Memorystore or Cloud Run in a subnet; Azure services behind a private endpoint in a subnet), and kinds were added: GCP folder, Private Service Connect, IAP, Apigee, AlloyDB, Filestore, Cloud Tasks, Cloud Build; Azure management group, availability zone, private endpoint, private DNS zone, Bastion, NAT Gateway, Container Apps, Container Instances, Logic Apps, managed identity; Kubernetes PersistentVolume and Role.
- The **Kind** search puts first the platform of the zone a box or zone goes in, then the platforms around it and in the picture, then the closest name ("container" before "Container Registry"); everyday words find kinds too (docker, k8s, db, redis, lb, bucket, queue…). Nothing found says so, and Enter then does nothing instead of closing the card.
- A box let go on another box on a map slides back and says so, as on state pictures; a box the draft adds or moves is always drawn above the ones that were there, never hidden under them (a box hidden that way looked lost, and a line drawn there went to the box on top).
- **Choose where a map's line meets its boxes.** As on state pictures: click a line (in draft mode, in its card) for **Leaves** and **Arrives**, or drag the dot at either end to another side; the map's router then only takes ways that leave and arrive there, or draws a curve between the two sides. Kept in the layout (`"@sides"`), so it is arrangement only, survives drags and the layout switch, and **Arrange again** clears it. While drafting, an end dragged onto or near another box joins the line to it (a line the draft drew changes its end; one in the plan is taken out and drawn again with its label, state and kind).
- **Profiles: what a box, zone or line is on a platform, starting with AWS.** On a map, a box, a zone or a line can have a kind (`aws:rds`, `aws:subnet`, `aws:event`) with its fields (`props`: engine, size, CIDR…). The card has a searchable **Kind** list grouped by platform; the icon follows the kind, the fields of that kind show under it, and the box or zone shows them ("RDS · postgres", "Subnet · private · 10.0.1.0/24"). The picture is tagged with the platforms it uses, kinds of line (event, VPC peering, IAM, replication) are drawn and named, and something placed where its kind does not usually sit (an S3 bucket in a subnet) gets a warning sign: it is never blocked, and anything without a kind is drawn freely. Profiles: AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, Huawei Cloud, On-prem (data center, rack, VLAN, DMZ, server, VM…), Container (host, compose project, network, container, volume) and Kubernetes (cluster, namespace, network policy, workloads, Service, Ingress, ConfigMap, Secret, PVC…); a map can mix them (an EKS cluster in an AWS VPC). They live in `lib/profiles.mjs`; `references/profiles.md` lists them for the agent.
- **Kubernetes is drawn on a map.** The `k8s` picture is gone: a cluster, its namespaces, workloads, configs and network policies are a map with the Kubernetes profile (`k8s:cluster`, `k8s:namespace`, `k8s:networkpolicy` zones; `k8s:deployment`, `k8s:cronjob`, `k8s:configmap`… boxes; `k8s:uses`, `k8s:selects` lines), so it has every map tool and mixes with AWS, Google Cloud or Azure around it. Plans with `kind: "k8s"` no longer load.
- **More kinds on every platform**, taken from each provider's product list (Google Cloud and Azure product pages, Huawei Cloud's product catalogue, the Kubernetes Gateway API, the Compose specification): Google Cloud 43 → 77 (managed instance groups, Bigtable, Dataproc, Kafka, Eventarc, Cloud NGFW, VPC Service Controls perimeters, Shared VPC…), Azure 40 → 86 (scale sets, SQL Managed Instance, Data Factory, Synapse, Fabric, Databricks, Log Analytics, Sentinel, Virtual WAN, AI Search…), Huawei Cloud 31 → 67 (Flexus, BMS, TaurusDB, DDS, DWS, CSS, ROMA Connect, Cloud Firewall, CBH…), On-prem 14 → 35 (sites, virtualization clusters and hypervisors, proxies, WAF, IDS/IPS, SIEM, backup, object storage, mainframe…; a server or VM runs Kubernetes too), Container 7 → 13 (Swarm, Podman pods, reverse proxy, secrets, configs, bind mounts; lines for `depends_on` and published ports), Kubernetes 18 → 35 (nodes and node pools, Gateway API gateways and routes, PDB, KEDA, cert-manager, ExternalSecret, Argo CD / Flux apps, service mesh…). Kinds of a cloud now draw their icon in that cloud's colours, and renamed products go by their current names (Azure Managed Redis, Managed Airflow) with the old ones still found by the search.
- The kind list in a box or zone card has a row of platforms. With nothing typed it shows every kind of the platform that fits where the box goes (before, only the first 60 kinds showed, so most platforms never did); a click shows another, and typing searches them all. Cards for a box, a zone or a line stay beside it when the page scrolls instead of staying put on the screen.
- A box or zone card needs a name: kept without one but with anything else filled in (a kind, a note, an icon), it stays open and says so, instead of quietly dropping the box. A card with nothing filled in still closes as not wanted.
- A kind on another platform's zone (a Compute Engine VM in an AWS subnet) says so on its sign ("in AWS, not Google Cloud") instead of naming zones it already sits in, and any warning sign opens what it means on a click or Enter.
- **Zones can share a name.** A map zone can have an `id` besides its `label`, so two regions can each hold a zone called "VPC". A zone is known by its `id`, or its label when it has none, in the draft and the layout; `walkboard check` says when two zones are known by the same one. Sketching a zone with a name in use keeps the name and gives it an id from where it is ("VPC (ap-southeast-7)"); renaming a zone to a name in use keeps its key. Before, the name was refused.
- **Copy, paste and duplicate in draft mode.** Select a box, zone or table and press ⌘C / ⌘V (Ctrl on other systems), or ⌘D for a copy beside it. On a map a zone comes with everything in it and the lines between those (a whole AZ with its subnets and servers); a table comes with its columns and foreign keys, named `<name>_copy`; state, decision, impact and flow pictures copy boxes (into the lane under the pointer). A paste goes into the zone, group or lane under the pointer, clear of what is there, as one undo. Each copy carries `copyOf`, so the agent sets it up like the original and asks only about what must differ.
- Opening or closing draft mode no longer moves the picture: the room its frame needs is taken from around the picture instead of pushing it 10 px right and down.
- Each kind of line has a look of its own (a mounted volume, a route and a backup no longer look like an event). With more than one platform on a map, the line card and the legend name the platform ("AWS: Event or message"), and the legend lists each look once. Enter keeps a line card wherever the focus is; a kind or a side chosen with a line's other changes is one undo. Escape, or letting go outside the window, takes back a line end being dragged. A side in `layout` that is not left, top, right or bottom is ignored instead of breaking the picture, and a draft change the server refuses says so instead of retrying quietly.
- **A review the agent can read at once.** Each edit says what it changes from (`was`), each kind its name (`kindName`: "Lambda (AWS)"), a line whose end was dragged to another box which line it replaces (`replaces` on its `addLink`), and `warnings` lists what the draft puts where its kind does not usually sit. The picture's `moved` no longer lists the layout's own keys (`@sides`, `@fixed`), and a sketched box carries its name. `walkboard draft` prints the same.
- `walkboard <command> --help` prints that command's options (and `walkboard --help` exits 0). The agent's guide says `props` in a change is the whole set, what `zone`, `parent` and `at` mean on a map, and that Kubernetes is a map with its profile.
- On a map placed **By hand**, a zone's title stays on one line under its own boxes: its kind tag, then the name, are cut short (whole on hover) instead of wrapping under the boxes or widening the zone over its neighbours.
- Opening the side panel (clicking a box) no longer rearranges the picture: it keeps the width it was laid out at and scrolls sideways while the panel is open, and the box clicked stays where it was on screen. Before, a narrower column wrapped a map's zones into another order and the box clicked could end up at the bottom. Closing the panel (✕ or Escape) keeps it there too, instead of the page jumping up.
- A new box's name field suggests names that fit the picture: states on a state picture, questions, steps or results on a decision picture (following the kind chosen), components on flow and impact pictures, targets on Kubernetes, steps on a plan's own steps.
- **Choose what a decision box is.** On a decision picture the box card (adding or editing) has **◆ Question / ▭ Step / ● End**, and the box takes that shape as you pick. An end is rounded and tagged **end**. Kept in the view's `shapes` (`setShape` in the draft); without it a box is drawn from its lines as before. A question with fewer than two answers, or an end with a line out, says so on the box. **? How to read this picture** shows the three kinds and how to make a decision picture.
- **Start and end in the box card.** While sketching a state or decision picture, the box card (double-click a box, the edit tool, or a new box) has two switches: **Start** and, on a state picture, **End** (`setStart`, `setEnd`). Ends have an **end** tag on the picture beside **start**, a decision picture now shows where it starts, and **? How to read this picture** says what both mean.
- **Lines meet boxes on the sides that face.** On state, decision and impact pictures, a line to a box straight below, above or to the left, with nothing between, runs straight from one edge to the facing one instead of going round by the right-hand side.
- **Choose where a line meets a box.** Click a line on a state, decision or impact picture (in draft mode too, in its card) for **Leaves** and **Arrives**: Auto or one of the four sides of each box. Or drag the dot that shows at either end of a line pointed at: the box shows the middle of its four sides, the nearest one lights up and the line follows; let go on another box while drafting and the line joins that box instead (a line the draft drew just changes its end; one in the plan is taken out and drawn again with its words), with the box lit and a guide line while dragging; a box near the pointer counts too, shows its four sides with the nearest lit, and the line joins it there; outside draft mode it stays and says to press Draft changes first. It shows as you pick and is kept with where the boxes are (`layout`, `sides`), so it is only arrangement: the plan does not change, undo takes it back and **Arrange again** clears it.
- A line on a state, decision or impact picture keeps its shape once boxes hold still: one to a box on a lower row still runs along the gap above that row, instead of arcing over the picture or bracketing round it after the first draft change or drag. Several such lines to boxes at the left edge no longer go past it and get cut off.
- **State, decision and impact pictures hold still.** From the first draft change on, nothing already on the picture moves: a new box stays where you clicked, boxes can be dragged (in and out of draft mode, saved in `layout`), and **Arrange again** lays the picture out from the plan. A box that grows moves what it reaches down; a box the agent just added starts on free ground. Lines take their shape from where the boxes are. Kubernetes keeps its fixed columns, where adding a box already moved nothing.

## 0.8.0

- **Much faster pages.** Lines were recalculated with a slow browser call: a big map redrew at about 2 frames a second while dragging, and now keeps up with the screen. The page no longer redraws when nothing changed, and a new box pulses three times instead of forever.
- **Undo and redo when arranging.** Buttons appear after the first drag, on maps and data models; ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y) work too. Going back to the automatic layout can be undone.
- **Boxes stay on top.** A box can no longer disappear behind a zone, and what you moved last lies over the rest.
- **Choosing in design mode.** Every tab can be chosen: a picture no option owns offers **Choose this picture**, and the agent is told which one. Supporting pictures (`"option": "<id>"` on a view) sit under their option. `check` warns about a picture no option owns, and the guide tells the agent to keep revised designs as options.
- The confirm panel no longer lets the chat run over the form: list, chat, then the form at the bottom. Confirm buttons line up.
- **Drag on map pictures.** Move a box, or a whole zone by its title; arrow keys move a selected box; the place is saved and **Lay out automatically** puts it back.
- **Landscape pages in PDF.** A big picture takes a landscape page of its own when that makes it larger. Check the print preview if your browser does not support mixed page orientation.
- Thai text uses a looped face where the computer has one, easier to read at small sizes.

- **Pictures for cloud architects.** Icons for Huawei Cloud and on-prem (data center, VMware, F5, Oracle, NAS, AD), for ExpressRoute, Direct Connect, Cloud Interconnect, VPN and firewalls. A `standby` line for a backup path. A big map keeps its zones in a row: over 12 boxes or 4+ top-level zones it uses smaller boxes, uses the whole window, and wraps only when it still does not fit. Lines also leave or enter a box from the side when another box is in the way.
- **PDF fixes.** A picture with lines stays whole on one page, scaled to fit: before, tall ones lost their boxes to the next page. Lines now meet their boxes on paper. A diff prints its change list with the first picture.
- A wide diff shows Before or After at a time, swapped in place. Sequences with 6+ actors and impact pictures fit without scrolling sideways. The line a move travels on is solid, so it never looks like a "not ready" dashed line, and its label no longer hides the box it reaches.
- When most boxes are guesses, each gets a small ❓ and the picture says it once. A score can be `"basis": "judgment"` when there is no document to cite. `check` warns when a tab would show a bare id, and the guide says to `title` each view; boxes that are not code need no `file`.
- **Stories in pictures.** A step can say what travels: `moves` sends a label along the line between two boxes each time the step opens, `keep` leaves it on the box it reached, `show` puts a few lines inside a box. Play holds each step long enough to read it, and moves wait, travel slowly and rest so you can follow them. On paper, moves print under their step.
- Big map pictures: `"stack": true` stacks what a zone holds, over 12 boxes the page uses smaller ones and keeps top-level zones in one row, and lines look for the nearest gap first.
- Kubernetes pictures: a line that would run through a box, or between workloads stacked one above the other, now goes around.
- Thai labels keep technical terms as engineers say them (commit message, block, Input/Output, Snapshot) and say what a thing is where they were vague.
- Map pictures route lines around boxes: below, above or beside them, each in its own lane, when the straight way would cross another box.
- A box taken out of the plan keeps its conversation: 🗑 in the conversations list, with its name, still readable (and in exports with chat). Typing there continues in the whole plan's chat, naming the box. The agent says why in the whole plan's chat before taking out a box someone talked about.

## 0.7.0

- **Move tables yourself.** On a data model picture, drag a table by its title or a group by its label; the place is saved in plan.json, so exports and other browsers keep it, and the agent is not woken. Arrow keys move a selected table; **Lay out automatically** puts everything back.
- **Groups** frame tables that belong together: ask the agent to group them.
- Columns that point at another table show FK, beside PK when a table shares another's key (1:1). A line between tables stacked one above the other loops beside them instead of crossing one.
- The comparison says what its dots are: "●●○ = weight · click to change".
- Chat opens on the newest message and stays there when unfolded, widened or resized, until you scroll up; also on panels with only a chat (the whole plan, an option), where the box to type in now stays in view.

## 0.6.0

- Design pages: every guess has **Confirm** and **Not true**; a constraint can name the criteria it decides (`affects`), which show ❓ until it is confirmed; a cell can show the figure behind its rating (`value`, e.g. a monthly price); options that fail a must show ✗ on their tab; a table's panel lists the columns it stores again and which are kept on purpose.
- PDF: a design opens with the choice (or recommendation) and why, why not the others and which boxes are guesses, then the comparison with weights as numbers; journeys print as numbered stops; explanations print the cited lines; an exported file with chat prints the conversation.
- Side panel: approving is sent once from either button; your turn and the suggested commits sit with the chat; the panel follows the agent to its next step; ⤢ keeps your place in the chat and is gone on phones, where the panel is a sheet with a grabber and a dimmed page behind it.
- `check` also fails when an anchor is no longer at the start of a cited range, a highlight is outside the lines shown, a title is blank, a list has an empty item, or a column key is not text. `set` keeps `[skip ci] …` as text.
